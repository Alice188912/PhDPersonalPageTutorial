## Teaching Philosophy

I believe teaching should encourage students to think critically and independently, while also making complex ideas accessible and intellectually rewarding. I value close reading, careful argument, and a supportive classroom environment in which students feel confident to ask questions and develop their own interpretive voices.

My teaching approach combines structured guidance with open discussion, especially in courses focused on literary analysis, cultural interpretation, and research methods.
