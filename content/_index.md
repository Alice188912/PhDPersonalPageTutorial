I am a PhD researcher in Comparative Literature at the University of Amsterdam, where my work focuses on modern selfhood, modern horror, and post-impressionist aesthetics in Virginia Woolf and Eileen Chang.

My research sits at the intersection of literary studies, comparative literature, visual culture, and feminist aesthetics. I am especially interested in modernism, Chinese modernity, and the relationship between word and image in shaping literary form and subjectivity.

My broader academic interests include modernity, affect, visuality, intermediality, and the gendered politics of aesthetic experience.

> “I am interested in how literature, image, and modern consciousness reshape one another.”
